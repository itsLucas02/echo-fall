# Echofall: Clockwork Run — Scope

## Goal

A four-chapter, mobile-playable browser platformer where the Time Echo — a 7-second recording of the player's movement — is the key to every puzzle, obstacles diversify each chapter, and five enemy species (including a stompable warden mini-boss) make the city feel alive.

## Player Experience

The player is a courier automaton carrying the last time core through a clockwork Malaysia: the arrival wards, the Batu caverns, the Cameron tea terraces, and the Merdeka ascent. They run, jump, dodge crushers and pendulums, ride collapsing planks and spring mushrooms, chain relay plates before their charge fades, follow lamp sequences, route their echo through echo-only resonators, power lifts from afar, and feed their echo to hunters as a decoy. Progress, best times, and shard records persist between sessions.

## Included

- Four data-driven chapters with distinct palettes, devices, hazards, and enemy mixes
- Keyboard controls plus automatic on-screen touch controls (move pads, jump, echo, pause, fullscreen)
- FIT scaling to any screen with a landscape recommendation on portrait phones
- Time Echo recording/replay, echo-only resonator switches, echo decoy AI, echo projectile absorption
- Hold gates, dual-lock vault, timed shutters, decaying relay chains, sequence locks, warden guard gates, plate-powered horizontal/vertical lifts
- Crushers, pendulums, crumbling platforms, bounce mushrooms, moving platforms, wind zones, spikes
- Crawler, flyer, spitter, charger, and warden (3-HP mini-boss) enemies
- Chapter select, unlock progression, per-chapter best time and shard records, par-time ranks
- Procedural ambience and action-specific sound with mute control
- Buffered jumps and coyote time without double jump
- Unit tests for level geometry, device rules, echo sampling, and persistence

## Deferred

- Bosses beyond the warden, power-ups, multiple simultaneous echoes
- Gamepad support, accounts, leaderboards
- Level editor and procedural generation
